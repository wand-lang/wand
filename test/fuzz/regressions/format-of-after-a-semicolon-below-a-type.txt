`of` is an ordinary name, so `type P = U; of 1` is a declaration and
then a statement that applies `of` to `1`. `wand f` wrote the statement
on the line below the declaration, at its column. The parser then read
that `of` as the OCaml spelling `U of Int` and refused it, so the output
did not parse.

The parser now refuses `of` only where it continues the declaration:
on the same line, or on a line indented past it. The fuzzer reached it
from test/wand/test_generics.wand through swap-lines, insert-fragment
and delete-span. The mutant was shrunk by hand to the one line here.
