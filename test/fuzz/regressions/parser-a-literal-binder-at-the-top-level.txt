fixed in:  lib/parser.ml, the top-level `let`'s pattern branch

A top-level binding whose binder is a literal in brackets:

  let(0)=0

The top level took a binding as an item of its own when the binder began
with a bracket, a brace or `_`, and as a name otherwise. A literal was
neither, so `let 0 = 0` fell through to the expression parser and became a
`let` with no body. The brackets kept `let (0) = 0` an item; the formatter
drops brackets around a literal, and the next pass read the result as an
expression and wrote `(let 0 = 0; ())`. The two passes disagreed.

A literal now opens the pattern branch, as `_` does.
